# ASAR illustration assets

The live interface uses the same original illustration language across onboarding, empty states, trust, achievements, community, success and authentication: warm geometric surfaces, rounded orbit shapes and the Lucide icon set. These scenes are composed from the centralized tokens in `design-tokens/tokens.json`, so they remain crisp at 200% zoom, lightweight and consistent across Russian and Kazakh layouts.

Future raster artwork should follow `docs/brand-guidelines.md`: respectful scenes of mutual help, people of different ages and abilities shown naturally, no stock-photo stereotypes, no pity-driven framing, and no personal information in the artwork.
